## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-integrity, institutional public portal for the Karo Regency Government. The visual language blends rigorous public-sector accountability with contemporary GovTech precision. The audience encompasses citizens, regional auditors, journalists, and public servants who require rapid, unequivocal access to budget, agricultural, infrastructural, and transport metrics.

The aesthetic fuses **Corporate / Modern GovTech** with **Editorial Precision**:
- High legibility, institutional restraint, and structural calm replace standard bureaucratic clutter.
- Clean white and slate-tinted canvas structures allow civic indicators, fiscal figures, and legal disclosures to stand out without decorative distraction.
- Visual anchors communicate reliability, state stewardship, and forward-looking governance through structured data presentation, crisp borders, and disciplined visual hierarchies.

## Colors

The palette is tuned strictly for accessibility (exceeding WCAG AAA for key body text and AA+ for interactive elements) and context-specific civic communication:

- **Primary (`#059669`, Emerald Green):** Symbolizes the agricultural heritage of Karo, ecological vitality, active infrastructure approvals, and verified transparency metrics.
- **Secondary (`#0f172a`, Deep Slate Navy):** The structural bedrock. Used for primary typography, dominant header framing, table schemas, and authoritative navigation surfaces.
- **Tertiary (`#d97706`, Warm Ochre/Amber):** Represents fiscal cautions, pending approvals, municipal revisions, and warm border accents across card boundaries.
- **Neutral (`#64748b`, Muted Slate):** Governs secondary labels, metadata captions, horizontal divider rules, and structural iconography.

### Semantic & Surface Rules
- **Base Canvas (`#f8fafc` / Slate-50):** Provides a calm, low-glare reading environment across extended audits.
- **Card/Container Surface (`#ffffff`):** Pure white elevates interactive modules and data tables off the base canvas.
- **Subtle Warm Border (`#e2e8f0` with a `#cbd5e1` active hover):** Enforces modular clarity without visual weight.
- **Verification Green (`#047857` text on `#ecfdf5` surface):** Denotes legally audited and finalized data entries.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy separates narrative civic communication from tabular fiscal telemetry:

- **Primary Interface Font (Inter):** Deployed for all page titles, narrative releases, modal content, and form fields. It provides neutral, uncompromised structural geometry optimized for dense information architectures.
- **Civic Data & Ledger Font (JetBrains Mono):** Applied systematically to budget figures, project codes, transaction hashes, dates, and verification status badges. Tabular figures prevent layout shifts during live data re-sorting.
- **Rhythm & Scanning:** Section headers employ negative letter-spacing for tight visual presence, while data labels utilize tracked uppercase monospace text for instant legibility during public audits.

## Layout & Spacing

The portal relies on a **12-column responsive fluid grid** pinned inside a max-width institutional frame of `1280px`, preventing excessive horizontal eye travel on ultra-wide monitors.

### Breakpoints & Layout Adapters
- **Desktop (>= 1024px):** 12 columns with `2rem` gutters and `3.5rem` outer page margins. Audit tables, key performance summaries, and departmental rosters appear in multi-column dashboards.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns with `1.5rem` gutters and `2rem` page margins. Secondary sidebars collapse into sticky bottom trays or accordion segments.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns with `1rem` gutters and `1rem` canvas margins. Complex fiscal tables convert to modular card lists with horizontal micro-scroll for budget line items.

### Spacing Principles
- Vertical rhythm follows an exact 4px/8px incremental cadence.
- Component internal padding uses compact steps (`space-sm` for table cells and chips, `space-lg` for card bodies) to maximize the amount of transparent data visible per viewport.

## Elevation & Depth

This system intentionally rejects heavy, dark drop-shadows and skeuomorphic gradients in favor of **low-contrast architectural outlines and layered tonal containment**:

- **Ground Level (Canvas):** Set to Slate-50 (`#f8fafc`). Non-interactive backdrop.
- **Tier 1 (Cards, Tables, KPI Modules):** Pure white (`#ffffff`) bounded by a hairline border (`1px solid #e2e8f0`). The slight warm undertone in the border prevents the clean white from feeling clinical.
- **Tier 2 (Interactive Floating Elements, Dropdowns, Flyouts):** Supported by a micro-elevation shadow: `0 4px 12px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)` wrapped with a fine border (`1px solid #cbd5e1`).
- **Tier 3 (Active Focus & Critical Alerts):** Expressed not through elevation, but through an explicit `2px` focus ring in Emerald Green (`#059669`) with an offset of `2px` white space for accessibility assurance.

## Shapes

The design system enforces a **Soft geometry (`roundedness: 1`)** standard. 

- **Containers, Cards, Panels:** Configured with `0.375rem` (6px) to `0.5rem` (8px) corners. This avoids the harshness of zero-radius brutalism while preserving an authoritative, legal-grade structure unlike consumer entertainment apps.
- **Data Badges & Status Chips:** Bounded by `0.25rem` (4px) micro-radii to maintain the appearance of physical municipal audit tags.
- **Form Controls & Action Buttons:** Configured at uniform `0.375rem` (6px), aligning precisely with nested table grids.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Triggers
- **Primary Action (Gov Verification/Export):** Solid `#059669` fill with pure white typography (`Inter`, 600 weight). Hover state shifts to `#047857`. Focus state adds an emerald contour ring.
- **Secondary Action (Filter/Inspect):** Slate-50 background, `#0f172a` text, wrapped in a 1px `#cbd5e1` stroke. Hover shifts to `#f1f5f9`.
- **Destructive/Flagging:** Soft rose-50 surface (`#fff1f2`), `#be123c` text, and a fine `#fecdd3` border.

### Status Badges & Transparency Chips
- Built using `label-badge` (`JetBrains Mono`, 11px uppercase).
- **Approved/Realized:** `#ecfdf5` background, `#047857` text, `1px solid #a7f3d0`.
- **Under Audit/Revision:** `#fffbeb` background, `#b45309` text, `1px solid #fde68a`.
- **Legislative Pending:** `#f1f5f9` background, `#334155` text, `1px solid #cbd5e1`.

### Civic Data Tables (SPJPP Core)
- **Header:** Sticky `#0f172a` surface with crisp white text, or soft `#f8fafc` surface with uppercase Slate-600 labels (`Inter`, 12px, 600 weight).
- **Rows:** Alternating subtle zebra striping (`#ffffff` and `#fcfdfe`), separated by fine horizontal lines (`1px solid #f1f5f9`).
- **Numbers & Currency Values (IDR):** Right-aligned, formatted in `JetBrains Mono` with consistent tabular figures for visual auditing.

### Form Inputs & Search Filters
- **Input Fields:** Crisp white background, `1px solid #cbd5e1` outline, `Inter` 14px regular body text. Placeholder in `#94a3b8`.
- **Active Focus:** Instant border color transition to `#059669` with a subtle `3px` emerald tint glow (`rgba(5, 150, 105, 0.12)`).

### Metric Cards (APBD / Infrastructure Realization)
- White container framed in `1px solid #e2e8f0`.
- Distinct top border accent: `3px solid #059669` for agricultural and transport development, and `3px solid #0f172a` for administrative general funds.
- Key figures set in `code-data-lg` (`JetBrains Mono`, bold, slate-900) flanked by micro trend indicators.
